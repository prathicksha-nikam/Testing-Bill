Android & iOS Setup Guide

Android (Direct Bluetooth ESC/POS):

Open this app in Google Chrome, tap Connect at the top, pair your 58mm/80mm thermal printer, and tap Save & Print via Bluetooth. Install to Home Screen via Chrome menu → Add to Home screen.

iPhone / iPad (iOS Safari):

Apple Safari blocks direct Web Bluetooth. On iOS, tap Save & PDF / AirPrint to print directly via AirPrint or share the receipt to your printer's companion app (e.g., RawBT / Thermer / Star / Epson) or WhatsApp. Tap Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen to install as an app.
